About Us

At Candid Coconut, we are committed to delivering the purest, most natural coconut products sourced directly from India’s finest coconut farms. We supply premium-grade Semi-Husked Coconuts, Fully-Husked Coconuts, 100% Organic Sulphur-Free Copra, and Cold-Pressed Coconut Oil, carefully processed to retain their authentic taste and nutritional benefits.

We proudly supply across India and export worldwide, serving industries that value purity — including cold-pressed oil mills, herbal and natural oil manufacturers, soap producers, and Ayurvedic medicine companies.
